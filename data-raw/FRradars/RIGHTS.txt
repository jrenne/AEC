FRradars: compact teaching extraction of Renne (October 2026),
The Effects of Speed Cameras on Injury Crashes in France.
Data release v1.1.0: https://github.com/jrenne/accidents-radars-france
Replication: https://github.com/jrenne/accidents-radars-replication
Extraction: 21 October 2026. Script/input SHA-256 values are frozen in
upstream-sha256.json. Counts/geometry refer to the article's 2 km network
reach, 100 m lateral tolerance, balanced first-year sample, 75% reach rule.

AEC transformations: preserve the full 406-stack baseline risk set, retain
639 distinct sites and integer counts in three 12-month windows, and omit
raw personal records, coordinates, geometry and unused covariates. The rows
are repeated site comparisons, not independent sites. The sources are not
represented as endorsing AEC or the course.
