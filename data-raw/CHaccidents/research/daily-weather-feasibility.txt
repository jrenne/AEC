Zurich daily accidents and weather: feasibility check, 2026-10-22

Outcome: no publicly redistributable Zurich accident source with exact dates was found in the official city, canton and GIS catalogues checked. Do not construct accident dates from weekday/month/year. No course or packaged data changed.

Creative Commons Attribution, required credit Bundesamt fuer Strassen ASTRA. Year, month, weekday, hour; no exact day/date.

https://ckan.opendata.swiss/api/3/action/package_show?id=polizeilich-registrierte-verkehrsunfalle-im-kanton-zurich-seit-2011
CSV inspected directly (header and first row, not full download):
https://daten.statistik.zh.ch/ogd/daten/ressourcen/KTZH_00000718_00001783.csv
Header has AccidentYear, AccidentMonth, AccidentWeekDay, AccidentHour, but no exact date/day-of-month. Resource-level rights and license are https://opendata.swiss/terms-of-use#terms_by (source attribution). Dataset-level license_id is null: inspect resource-level fields rather than treating that null as a prohibition.
GIS catalogue https://www.geolion.zh.ch/geodatensatz/5049 describes a five-year extract of the anonymised federal OGD model; not evidence of additional exact dates.
Official contact for possible daily aggregate release: vpsa@kapo.zh.ch. No message sent. Request city-day injury accident counts including zero days, 2011-2025, plus explicit permission to redistribute in AEC, rather than accident-level exact locations/timestamps.

Daily historical CSV header and first row successfully read:
https://data.geo.admin.ch/ch.meteoschweiz.ogd-smn/sma/ogd-smn_sma_d_historical.csv
Columns include reference_timestamp, tre200d0 (mean temperature), rre150d0, rka150d0. First row 1864-01-01. This was not a complete missingness/coverage audit.
Alignment warning: https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/general/download documents UTC reference dates and 06-to-06 precipitation days for standard precipitation totals. Inspect parameter metadata or aggregate subdaily observations to Europe/Zurich civil days before any daily accident join. Do not simply equate all reference dates with local calendar days.

Consequence for teaching: daily weather-accident boxes cannot yet be constructed honestly from identified open sources. Monthly weather matching remains possible, or obtain an explicitly redistributable daily accident-count release. No estimates claimed.
